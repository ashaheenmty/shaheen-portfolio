Building TikTok LIVE Creator Networks Globally

12 markets. One global social framework.

TikTok LIVE Creator Networks operated across different markets with different approaches to positioning, communication and social presence, and the model itself was often misunderstood by agencies, creators and other industry stakeholders. The business needed a clearer, more scalable way to communicate what Creator Networks are, how the model works, and why agencies and creators should care.

I played a major role in developing the social strategy and positioning approach across 12 international markets, from the initial business case and internal alignment through channel creation, positioning, content strategy, growth planning, governance, agency coordination and ongoing optimization.

What we built

  • Global social presence across 12 markets
  • Unified positioning and messaging direction
  • Governance framework
  • Market-specific localization
  • Agency and production workflows
  • Performance measurement frameworks

The content strategy

  • What Creator Networks are and how the model operates
  • How agencies and creators can benefit
  • How Creator Networks support creator growth
  • Common misconceptions and ecosystem opportunities

The impact

Moved Creator Networks' social presence from fragmented market-level communication toward a structured global framework, positioning social as a key education and communication channel connecting Creator Networks with agencies, talent managers, creators and the broader creator-economy audience.

~180 planned content pieces / month across 12 markets

When I joined TikTok, TikTok LIVE's social presence in MENA needed to become more structured, professional and aligned with the wider TikTok brand. I contributed significantly to shaping TikTok LIVE MENA's social presence across TikTok and Instagram: content strategy, editorial planning, creative direction, campaigns, creator content, events, education and community engagement.

Content directions

  • Creator education and creator stories
  • Business use cases and LIVE opportunities
